The BloomVault Philosophy

ABOUT

BloomVault Farms is a Virginia-based genetics project focused on preservation, terpene identity, and memorable breeding work.

“The goal is not to release endless names. The goal is to create genetics people remember.”

What BloomVault Represents

BloomVault was built around the idea of a genetics archive — a place where standout profiles are preserved, refined, and documented instead of lost over time.

Projects are selected around strong terpene direction, recognizable identity, and long-term breeding value. Gas, skunk, sour, citrus, OG, dessert, and fuel-forward lines all play a role in shaping the future catalog.

The Direction

The BloomVault catalog aims to balance old-school character with modern appeal. Some projects focus on preservation. Others push into new combinations designed to stand out visually, aromatically, and structurally.

Core Focus
Preservation, terp identity, memorable releases, and long-term foundational breeding.
Based In
Virginia, USA — building a recognizable regional breeding identity.
